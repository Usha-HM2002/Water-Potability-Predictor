💧 Water Potability Predictor

Predict if water is safe to drink using advanced machine learning models.

📖 About This Project

This project uses machine learning to evaluate water quality and predict whether a sample is potable (drinkable) or not. By analyzing chemical, physical, and environmental factors, the system helps ensure safer access to clean drinking water.

📊 Dataset Information

  • ✔️ Source: Kaggle - Water Potability Dataset
  • ✔️ Total Samples: 3,276 water samples
  • ✔️ Features: pH, Hardness, Solids, Chloramines, Sulfate, Conductivity, Organic Carbon, Trihalomethanes, Turbidity
  • ✔️ Target: Potability (1 = Safe, 0 = Not Safe)

⚙️ How It Works

1️⃣ Enter or upload water sample details

2️⃣ Our ML model analyzes 9 chemical parameters

3️⃣ The system predicts if the water is Potable or Not Potable
